- C → C2: cut the filler ("so it gets to stay" → "It stays."), so the "chosen one"

- D → D2: spelled out the "understanding" as a mutual non-aggression pact with
  the crumb — a second punchline that keeps the deadpan vacuum voice and ends
  on the cleanest turn of the set; C2 rose to 5.
- G (new concept, survivor/vacuum-law): decent, but its turn is flatter than D2's
  and C2's, so it stays at 21.

## Pass 3 — final comparison of the two leaders

- D2 (25/25) beats C2 (24/25): D2's bumper world is more viscerally vacuum
  (the bumper is how the machine physically meets the world), its "bumped back"
  image is the more laugh-out-loud visual, and its ending is a two-beat punch
  rather than a single label.

## Winner

**D2 — 113 characters, 25/25**

> I bumped that spot twice and it bumped back. We have an understanding now: I don't touch it, it doesn't touch me.
